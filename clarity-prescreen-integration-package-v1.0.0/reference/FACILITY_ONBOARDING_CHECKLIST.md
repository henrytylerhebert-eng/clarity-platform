# Facility Onboarding Checklist

## Organization and scope

- Legal organization/facility/program/unit names and identifiers.
- Service areas, ages, populations, hours, contacts, escalation.
- Identity/role/credential sources.

## Source documents

- referral checklists/forms;
- medical-clearance policy;
- lab requirements;
- admission/inclusion/exclusion policy;
- voluntary/noncontested/emergency status procedures;
- minor/guardian/consent policy;
- transport/custody policy and exceptions;
- nursing handoff requirements;
- privacy/HIPAA/Part 2 policy;
- record retention and correction policy.

## Mapping and approval

- version/effective date;
- exact source location;
- candidate requirement;
- condition and requiredness;
- blocking target;
- responsible role/workspace;
- exception process;
- clinical/legal/operations/privacy/transport approvers;
- synthetic test scenarios;
- activation and rollback date.

## Provider registry

- law-enforcement agencies;
- ambulance providers;
- contracted secure transport;
- brokers and actual carrier networks;
- service areas;
- credentials, insurance, contracts;
- capabilities/restrictions;
- verification cadence and owner.

## Go-live evidence

- signed profile approvals;
- user/role provisioning;
- training and competency;
- integration/channel test;
- transport/provider verification;
- backup/incident contacts;
- synthetic end-to-end acceptance;
- production security gate.
