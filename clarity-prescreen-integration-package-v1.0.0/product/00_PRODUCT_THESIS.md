# Product Thesis

## Working product name

**Clarity Prescreen & Referral Orchestration**

## Thesis

Behavioral-health crisis referrals fail operationally before they fail clinically. The story is repeated across calls, paper forms, texts, faxes, PDFs, and handoffs. The patient’s willingness, orientation, medical status, legal authority, facility requirements, transport authority, and packet evidence are often discussed in separate channels without a shared case record.

Clarity Prescreen creates one governed case spine from first contact through Central Intake, authorized review, facility response, transport, and custody handoff.

## Core question

> What do we know, where did it come from, what remains unresolved, who has authority to decide, and what must happen next?

## Primary outcomes to enable

These are product outcomes, not measured improvement claims:

- Field teams ask a consistent set of questions without carrying every facility’s paper form.
- Assessors review and correct their work before attestation.
- Central Intake receives a structured prescreen and packet rather than reconstructing the case by phone.
- Missing documents and unresolved questions have owners and due times.
- Authorized clinicians, legal authorities, and facilities see the evidence supporting the request.
- Transport and custody are linked to the active legal authority and destination.
- Facility-specific requirements remain configurable, versioned, sourced, and approved.
- The full history remains available for audit and operational learning.

## Primary users

1. Crisis-response and law-enforcement officers.
2. Private-hospital prescreen assessment staff.
3. Municipal, parish, regional, or state crisis-assessment teams.
4. Community behavioral-health prescreen teams.
5. Central Intake coordinators.
6. Authorized accepting practitioners.
7. Sending and receiving nurses.
8. Medical-clearance providers.
9. Transport and custody personnel.
10. Facility administrators, clinical leaders, legal/compliance owners, and onboarding specialists.

## Product boundary

The module supports documentation and orchestration. It does not replace:

- emergency-response protocol;
- clinician judgment;
- capacity determination;
- legal instrument execution;
- medical clearance;
- admission acceptance;
- placement authority;
- transport authority;
- consent law;
- hospital policy.

## Differentiator

The differentiator is not a longer assessment. It is the combination of:

- guided capture;
- source attribution;
- progressive disclosure;
- versioned review and attestation;
- referral packet readiness;
- structured communications and tasks;
- facility-policy configuration;
- legal/transport/custody traceability;
- system-agnostic integrations.
