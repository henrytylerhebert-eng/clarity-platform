## Transport-provider research baseline

| Organization | Publicly observed role | Production handling |
|---|---|---|
| Acadian Ambulance | Current public materials describe emergency, non-emergency, and specialized transport; LDH EMS provider materials list Acadian entities. | Treat as an ambulance candidate only after live entity, credential, service-area, contract, crew, destination, and capability checks. |
| Secure Patient Delivery | Its public contact page advertises a dedicated “PEC Transports” line. LPSC Docket T-38018 was open on 2026-07-19 and concerns proposed cancellation of Common Carrier Certificate No. 8647 for annual-report/fee noncompliance. | Treat as blocked/conditional for production selection until the docket outcome and all current credentials, insurance, contracts, local authority, and facility approvals are verified. An open cancellation proceeding is not the same as a final cancellation. |
| MediTrans | Current LDH transportation pages identify MediTrans as a Medicaid transportation broker/network coordinator. | Store the arranger separately from the actual assigned carrier; ordinary NEMT/broker status does not establish authority for a specific OPC/PEC/CEC trip. |
| Law enforcement | Executes OPC pickup and may transport under valid authority | Represent agency, officer, instrument, execution, custody, and handoff. |

## Required production rule

The product must never convert this static research register into a live provider-eligibility claim. A production dispatch decision requires a current credential check, instrument and jurisdiction match, sending/receiving approval, capability match, and documented policy authority.
