# Asset provenance — official Louisiana OBH forms

These five files are byte-identical copies of the official, currently-published Louisiana Department of Health (LDH) Office of Behavioral Health (OBH) forms. They are blank government form templates — no patient data, no PHI — and are used only as the static base layer that `render.ts` overlays with fillable AcroForm fields. **The visual/static page content of these files must never be edited, redrawn, or otherwise altered** — see the no-alteration rule in `docs/legal/LOUISIANA_OPC_PEC_CEC_FORM_VERIFICATION.md`. If LDH republishes a revised form, replace the file here, update this table, and re-verify the field-traceability matrix in that memo before changing `formTypes.ts`/`render.ts` to match.
