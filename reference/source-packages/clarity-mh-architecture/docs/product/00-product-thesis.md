# Product Thesis — Clarity MH / Blue Partner

## One-line definition

**Clarity MH is a guided crisis-intake and clinical-documentation workbench that turns one structured capture into four reviewed outputs: assessment, medical-necessity narrative, legal instrument, and referral packet.**

## The problem

Behavioral health crisis intake is fragmented across calls, CAD notes, ER intake, paper legal forms, payer portals, clinical notes, and referral calls. The result is slow, risky, and expensive:

- Families repeat the same story multiple times.
- Clinicians and officers under-document because they fear legal exposure.
- Hospitals reject or delay referrals because the packet is incomplete.
- Payers deny care because medical necessity is not clearly documented.
- Courts, DAs, coroners, and hospitals lack one defensible record.
- The treatment team loses the story after admission.

## The wedge

Clarity MH should not start as a statewide bed board or a proprietary criteria engine.

The defensible first wedge is:

> **Acute behavioral health intake + medical necessity + Louisiana legal instrument custody.**

This wedge is valuable because it sits before every downstream decision: admit, divert, transfer, authorize, discharge, or route to lower level of care.

## The four outputs

One guided capture produces:

1. **Clinical assessment** — presenting crisis, risk, MSE, substance use, medical suitability, collateral, functional impairment, level-of-care factors.
2. **Medical-necessity summary** — payer-ready narrative showing severity, intensity of service, failed lower LOC, and need for 24-hour care when applicable.
3. **Legal instrument** — OPC/PEC/CEC or voluntary status documentation, signed and hash-sealed when legally required.
4. **Referral packet** — receiving-facility-ready packet with acceptance receipt, decline reasons, missing-info requests, and custody ledger.

## Product personality

Clarity MH should behave like a calm expert intake coach:

- It asks what matters.
- It explains why it matters.
- It helps the user advocate for the patient.
- It catches missing facts before the patient is lost in the system.
- It never pretends to be the clinician.

## Strategic north star

**The legal record, clinical record, payer story, and routing packet should be generated from the same source-of-truth facts.**

No retyping. No shadow chart. No paper shuffle. No hidden reasoning.
