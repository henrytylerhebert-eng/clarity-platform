1.  Problem summary (what the meeting surfaced)
•  Clinical documentation is sparse, fragmented, and often locked in multiple silos (officer notes, CAD, ER intake, paper PECs). That prevents timely, defensible transfers to inpatient hospitals and to courts/DA.
•  Officers/clinicians are concerned about discoverability and legal exposure, so they under‑document or document in ways that are hard to trust later.
•  The manual chain of custody (paper PECs, CDs, burned discs, triplicate paper) is slow, error‑prone, and incompatible with modern DA/hospital workflows.
•  Mental‑health cases are high‑stakes (suicide risk, involuntary commitment). Lack of a reliable documentation pipeline can delay care or prevent acceptance by hospitals/receiving facilities.
•  For Louisiana specifically, local reporting and legal formats (PECs, OTPs) and hospital acceptance processes vary and require standardized, auditable submission.
2.  Mental‑health value proposition for Clarity / Blue Partner
•  Clarity reduces clinician/officer cognitive load by capturing structured intake, time‑stamped observations, and actionable “next steps” that align to risk triage (e.g., standardized suicide intent scoring).
•  For officers and clinicians, Clarity is framed as protective: well‑documented decisions reduce malpractice/disciplinary risk and improve patient outcomes (faster acceptance, better diversion).
•  For agencies and hospitals, Clarity creates a defensible, auditable record that speeds transfers, reduces repeat ER/booking cycles, and supports grant/funding claims for community crisis response.
3.  Critical features to solve chain of custody for PECs/OPCs/ECs
•  Structured intake templates mapped to Louisiana legal forms:
    •  Build configurable templates that mirror the required fields on LA PEC/Order of Protective Custody/Emergency Certificate so captures can be exported in the exact format hospitals and courts expect.
•  End‑to‑end digital chain of custody:
    •  Time‑stamped creation, edits, redactions, and transfers (WORM logs).
    •  Immutable audit log (who accessed, exported, transmitted, and redacted, with timestamps and source IP/user).
•  Secure electronic handoff with acceptance receipt:
    •  Push to receiving hospital/triage desk via secure channel (SFTP, encrypted API, or email with signed receipt) with automatic “accepted/declined” status and reason codes.
•  Document integrity & evidence preservation:
    •  Hashing of originals and exported artifacts to prove non‑tampering.
    •  Option to attach recorded interviews / body‑cam segments and link them into the case packet with timestamps and transcript extracts.
•  Automated redaction and controlled sharing:
    •  Redaction pipeline that flags PHI / privileged items and allows role‑based redaction before sharing externally.
    •  “Redaction by steward” workflow (e.g., Blue Partner redacts for UL/research but leaves full record to authorized agency).
•  DA / hospital‑ready packet generation:
    •  A single export that includes: structured PEC form, narrative summary, suicide risk scales, witness statements, media (secure links), and chain‑of‑custody metadata.
•  Verification & legal attestation:
    •  Quick e‑signature of the officer/clinician and timestamped attestation statements that match statutory requirements.
•  Interoperability with existing systems:
    •  LIBRS submission compatibility, CAD/SAP/EMR connectors, and ability to print the legal copy if required.
4.  Process design & operational controls (recommended workflow)
•  At scene / intake:
    •  Officer/clinician opens Clarity incident template → captures initial observations, risk score, and basic media (phone audio/photo).
    •  System auto‑attaches geolocation and timestamp metadata.
•  Pre‑transfer review:
    •  A designated clinical reviewer (EOC nurse / crisis clinician) reviews packet, attaches required medical notes, and triggers redaction/attestation.
•  Transfer:
    •  Packet sent via secure channel to receiving hospital. System requires an electronic acceptance response before transport starts or logs refusal for fallback steps.
•  Post‑transfer custody:
    •  Hospital confirms chain receipt; system records acceptance time and receiver identity. If hospital declines, system logs reasons and recommended next actions.
•  Archival & audit:
    •  All artifacts stored encrypted, indexed, and available to authorized legal/clinical reviewers. Exportable audit reports for DA, UL study, or grant audits.
