# 14 — Clinical & Operational Safety Hazard Register

**Audit date:** 2026-07-18. Prototype context: synthetic data only, client-side demo, no production use. Hazards are registered against the product these artifacts are heading toward; "Existing control" reflects what the repo has today.

| # | Hazard | Scenario | Potential harm | Existing control | Gap | Proposed mitigation | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| H-2 | Single-click execution of liberty-depriving instruments | "Sign, attest & seal certificate" (PEC) and "Execute certificate & freeze record" (CEC) fire immediately on one click — no review-and-confirm screen | Erroneous involuntary-commitment record; contradicts the project's own WCAG 3.3.4 requirement in the OPC/PEC/CEC review | Button disabled until form structurally valid (validation added 2026-07-17) | No confirmation/summary step; no explicit attestation of *who* is signing (no authenticated identity exists) | Review-and-confirm screen listing all entered data + deadlines before signature activates; block e-execution entirely until identity exists (wet-sign posture already documented) | **P0 for any real use; P1 to fix in prototype now** |

## Hazards explicitly well-controlled today (preserve)

- **28-day auto-release:** prohibited and absent; UI displays "medically necessary period" context. (H-5 family)
- **CEC independence:** same-examiner PEC→CEC blocked in both app validation and package validator, tested.
- **Telehealth role gate:** Act 148 role list enforced in both layers, tested.
- **Cross-tenant leakage:** gateway-level scoping with dedicated security test suites.
- **Synthetic-data discipline:** SECURITY.md rule 1; fixtures namespaced `synthetic-*`; mock cohort uses fictional sitcom characters and is segregated in a labeled training workspace.
