# Grant Opportunities for Crisis Coordination

**Scope:** Recent, historical, and forecasted federal grant opportunities relevant to law enforcement diversion, mobile crisis response, and behavioral health coordination.

---

## 1. Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA)

### Behavioral Health Mobile Crisis Team Partnerships (MCTP)
- **Funder:** SAMHSA
- **Status:** Recently Closed (Deadline: July 15, 2026)
- **Funding Opportunity Number:** SM-26-029
- **Eligibility:** States, local governments, behavioral health authorities.
- **Allowable Uses:** Establishing new or enhancing existing mobile crisis teams. Creating structured partnerships that reduce reliance on law enforcement.
- **Clarity Fit:** [Inference] Clarity could act as the coordination software that fulfills the "structured partnerships" requirement by linking law enforcement directly with the funded mobile crisis teams.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed Historical
- **Source:** [samhsa.gov/grants](https://www.samhsa.gov/grants)

---

## 2. Department of Justice (DOJ) / Bureau of Justice Assistance (BJA)

### Justice and Mental Health Collaboration Program (JMHCP)
- **Funder:** DOJ BJA
- **Status:** Recently Closed (Typically opens Spring each year; 2026 cycle closed)
- **Eligibility:** States, units of local government, federally recognized Indian tribes.
- **Allowable Uses:** Implementing co-responder models, Crisis Intervention Teams (CIT), and cross-system collaboration between criminal justice and mental health agencies.
- **Clarity Fit:** [Inference] Clarity fits perfectly into JMHCP's goal of "cross-system collaboration," specifically as the data-sharing and handoff infrastructure between police and behavioral health receiving centers.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed Historical / Forecasted for Spring 2027
- **Source:** [bja.ojp.gov/funding](https://bja.ojp.gov/funding)

### Connect and Protect: Law Enforcement and Behavioral Health Response Program
- **Funder:** DOJ BJA
- **Status:** Forecasted/Historical
- **Eligibility:** Law enforcement agencies partnering with mental health agencies.
- **Allowable Uses:** Designing and implementing law enforcement-behavioral health cross-system collaboration to improve public safety and health outcomes.
- **Clarity Fit:** [Inference] Funding could be used to procure coordination software that formalizes the "Connect and Protect" mandate.
- **Source:** [bja.ojp.gov/funding](https://bja.ojp.gov/funding)

---

## Summary of Opportunity

The federal funding landscape heavily prioritizes removing law enforcement from the primary role of mental health crisis response. Grants like SAMHSA's MCTP and BJA's JMHCP explicitly fund the *connective tissue* between police, 988, and behavioral health providers. Clarity is highly aligned with these goals.
