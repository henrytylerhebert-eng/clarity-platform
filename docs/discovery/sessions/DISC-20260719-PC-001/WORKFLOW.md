## Handoff Rules

- A facility response is a source fact recorded from a named human, not a
  recommendation generated by Clarity.
- Admission requires the accepted source handoff and explicit facility-owned
  timezone configuration.
- Pre-admission authorization readiness remains in its existing workstream.
- Post-admission UR is episode-owned and does not overwrite the pre-admission
  record.
- MAR processing records and routes medication information; it does not
  authorize medication orders.
- Financial readiness may expose gaps but does not block emergency clinical
  review.
