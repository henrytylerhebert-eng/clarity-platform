## Findings

### Verified

- Clarity contains versioned-document, reviewed-evidence, supersession,
  contradiction, audit, organization-scope, readiness, regulatory-tooling, and
  synthetic-training patterns.
- Those patterns are case-oriented. No operating-assurance product,
  organization-policy domain, consultant control plane, or reviewed-answer
  workflow exists.
- No application, schema, package, migration, test, or runtime file was changed
  in this product-definition phase.

### Documented

- The current-labeled portfolio proxy contains 91 client/engagement-like units:
  54.9% hospital/facility, 29.7% RHC, and 15.4% special projects.
- Visible delivery scopes are multi-family; reporting/monitoring, policy/SOP,
  survey/regulatory, quality/audit, governance, startup/licensure, training,
  facility/life-safety, infection prevention, and corrective action recur.
- The operations collection is predominantly shared consultant
  capability/reference material.
- Dated operating artifacts span 2019-2026, with substantial annual/version
  segmentation and repeated evidence, action, training, and closeout
  structures.

### Reported

- The owner wants clients to operate the product and consultants to supervise
  it.
- The owner states that the consulting tools, workflows, evidence methods, and
  plans of correction have been used since 2020 while building the business.
- The desired long-term capability includes question answering across
  regulation, policy, procedure, and client evidence.
- The current authorized phase is product definition and first-release
  scoping, not broad ideation or implementation.

### Inferred

- The product should be separate and adjacent to Clarity, with selective reuse
  reviewed later.
- The longitudinal corpus is a major product-intelligence asset for domain
  logic, templates, evaluation, tenant-private retrieval, and human-reviewed
  assistance after rights and privacy review.
- The defensible wedge is the inspectable
  authority-policy-SOP-evidence-answer-action lineage plus multi-client
  consultant oversight.
- Broad policy, LMS, credentialing, incident, and GRC replacement would compete
  in mature categories before the wedge is validated.

## Decisions

### Made

- OD-15 is resolved for Product Intelligence: client compliance/quality/program
  owner is the primary user; assigned consultant is the oversight user; the
  minimum job is one reviewed assurance answer or explicit gap.
- The owner accepts the underlying consulting method and value as established
  by longitudinal use. Product Intelligence no longer treats the method as an
  untested workflow hypothesis.
- Product Intelligence will treat evidence as evidence, never as automatic
  compliance.
- The first-release hypothesis is one facility, three actors, pre-seeded
  immutable references, one descriptive question, one evidence response, and
  one consultant-reviewed answer or explicit gap, plus one adversarial replay
  with a superseded or conflicting reference.
- Corrective-action management, closure, change propagation, multi-client
  portfolio control, Q&A retrieval, and exports are later capabilities, not
  part of the first release.
- The release validates only the reviewed-answer contract. Consultant leverage,
  portfolio differentiation, and production tenant isolation require separate
  later discovery.
- Historical artifacts are a candidate intelligence corpus, not automatically
  authorized model-training data.
