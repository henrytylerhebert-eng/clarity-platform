Safety And Truth Boundaries

This artifact is built for sharing, but it preserves the same product boundaries as the prototype.

Synthetic only

No PHI, no real facility data, no production identifiers.

Human review

Clinical, medical necessity, legal, placement, and authorization outputs remain draft or review-gated.

Counsel validation

Legal clocks and statutory language are placeholders until legal review.

No payer certainty

Benefits quotes are not payment guarantees and financial readiness does not block clinical workflow.

No outcome claims

Transfer timing, acceptance rates, and documentation quality improvements are No measurements found.

POC feedback first

Market fit, feature naming, and workflow ownership remain Unknown until stakeholder review.
