# NBC Sitcom Inpatient Admit Training Report

Status: Synthetic mock-use clone only.

This document is a fictitious Clarity training cohort. It uses public fictional-character names as role-play tokens so reviewers can practice the workflow without real PHI. It is not a clinical record, legal form, payer authorization, or medical-necessity certification.

## Global Guardrails

- All diagnoses are provisional training drafts and require clinician review.
- Legal status, hold language, and clock interpretation require counsel validation.
- Utilization-review summaries are draft support narratives only.
- No section may be copied into a real chart without qualified human review.
- No real PHI, real facility data, or real law-enforcement event is represented.

## Standard Clarity Process For Each Mock Admission

1. **Referral and field capture:** responder records scene facts, source, location, observed risk, patient statements, collateral, and unknowns.
2. **Central intake:** intake coordinator creates the case, confirms demographics token, benefits lane, safety level, and missing facts.
3. **Guided assessment:** clinician reviews mental status, risk, precipitating event, history, comorbidities, collateral reliability, and lower-level alternatives.
4. **Source-linked risk:** each risk finding links to field observation, collateral, ED note, or clinician observation.
5. **Medical-necessity draft:** severity of illness, intensity of service, functional impairment, and lower-level failure are summarized as draft support.
6. **Legal or voluntary status draft:** voluntary admission is preferred when capacity and consent are present; involuntary status is counsel-gated.
7. **Packet preview:** assessment, risk, insurance, legal status, medication concerns, and custody hash are assembled.
8. **Routing response:** receiving unit/facility accepts, declines, requests information, or waitlists with reason code.
9. **Bed and milieu decision:** charge nurse checks population fit, acuity, elopement, self-harm, aggression, medical support, and observation level.
10. **UR/chart draft:** reviewer prepares a chart-facing training summary and UR support statement for human review.

## Utilization Review Training Checklist

For each case, Oscar Martinez should verify that the draft packet contains:

- current symptoms and precipitating event;
- risk to self, others, grave disability, medical instability, elopement, or vulnerability;
- objective source references and collateral reliability;
- psychiatric, substance-use, medical, medication, and social history;
- functional impairment and safety-plan limitations;
- lower-level alternatives considered and why they are not adequate now;
- requested level of care and observation needs;
- initial treatment targets;
- discharge barriers and early discharge planning;
- payor and authorization notes;
- reviewer name, date/time, and draft status.
